How NeuralPresence Works

Three simple steps to fully automated attendance tracking.

01

Register Students

Capture student photos via webcam. Our AI extracts unique 128-dimensional face encodings for each individual.

02

Scan & Recognize

Students face the camera on arrival. The system matches their face against registered encodings in milliseconds.

03

Auto-Log Attendance

Attendance is instantly logged with timestamp and confidence score. Reports are generated automatically.
